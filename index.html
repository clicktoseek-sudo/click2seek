Technology

From raw footage
to research insight

01
Edge capture & preprocessing
Camera traps, underwater housings, and drone gimbals stream or store footage. Onboard microcontrollers apply denoising, colour correction, and frame selection before processing.
02
Domain-tuned neural inference
Our vision models are fine-tuned on carefully curated Indian subcontinent wildlife datasets — not generic ImageNet weights. Dramatically better performance in local habitat conditions.
03
Confidence scoring & audit trail
Every detection carries a confidence value and bounding metadata. Low-confidence detections are flagged for human review, ensuring research-grade reliability.
04
BiomeCloud aggregation & analysis
Structured data flows into BiomeCloud where it is time-indexed, cross-referenced against habitat maps, and surfaced as biodiversity metrics and exportable datasets.
About Us

Built for researchers, by problem-solvers

Click2Seek was founded in Bengaluru in 2020 with a single conviction: that computer vision could fundamentally change how humanity understands, monitors, and protects biodiversity. Recognised by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade under Startup India (DIPP67254), we operate at the intersection of deep learning and ecological science.

Our multidisciplinary team combines expertise in computer vision, embedded systems, marine biology, and wildlife ecology. We don't build generic AI — every model we release has been trained and validated in the specific environmental contexts where it will operate.

We collaborate with forest departments, universities, and conservation NGOs to ensure our tools are rigorous, interpretable, and genuinely useful to the people working to protect India's extraordinary natural heritage.
